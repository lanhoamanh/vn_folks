PROJECT PERSPECTIVES

THE FOUR LENSES

Examining supernatural tales and cultural traditions through four complementary viewpoints:

Oral Folklore

Living myths, nursery rhymes, and spiritual traditions passed down through generations across village hearths, rivers, and temples.

Historical Context

Environmental, agrarian, and social conditions in historic Vietnam that influenced how communities interpreted the unknown.

Human Perception

How human psychology processes nighttime fears, visual patterns in dark shadows, and protective narrative framing.

Modern Science

Insights from neurology, sleep biology, wildlife sensory systems, and astronomy that illuminate the physical world behind legends.
