THE ARCHIVE METHODOLOGY

THE FOUR LENSES

Moving beyond simplistic true-or-false binaries, VNFOLKS examines cultural traditions through multiple disciplined perspectives.

Oral Folklore

The living myths, rhymes, and spiritual traditions passed down through generations of village hearths, rivers, and temples.

Historical Context

The environmental, agrarian, and social realities of ancient Vietnam that shaped how communities responded to the unknown.

Human Psychology

Cognitive patterns, pareidolia, fear conditioning, and the universal human instinct to weave narratives around danger.

Modern Science

Insights from sleep neurology, ethology, and astronomy that illuminate the physical mechanisms behind ancient phenomena.
