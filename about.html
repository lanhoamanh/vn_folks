ABOUT THIS PROJECT

ABOUT VNFOLKS

“Exploring the intersection between cultural folklore and scientific understanding.”

“VN FOLKS began with a straightforward goal: to document Vietnamese supernatural legends and traditional lore while exploring the physical and psychological science behind them.

Across Vietnam, generations have shared stories about nighttime spirits, animal omens, celestial events, and protective household rituals. Some originated to explain natural phenomena before modern science, others served as community safety guidelines, and many evolved into cherished seasonal traditions.

This archive provides accessible, nuanced context for these tales without dismissing traditional beliefs or oversimplifying historical culture.”
PART 01

PROJECT VISION

Vietnamese folklore contains a rich tapestry of stories, yet digital resources in English often reduce these tales to generic horror tropes. VN FOLKS was created to present these legends with cultural depth, historical context, and scientific curiosity.

When early communities witnessed eclipses or experienced sleep immobility, they developed memorable narratives that brought people together. Documenting these stories preserves an essential aspect of Vietnamese heritage.

PART 02

FOLKLORE & SCIENCE

A core principle of this project is demonstrating that cultural folklore and empirical science can coexist thoughtfully.

For example, analyzing Bóng Đè through sleep cycle biology explains the neurological mechanism of muscle immobility during REM sleep, while folk customs reveal how communities provided psychological comfort to affected individuals. Examining both perspectives offers a fuller appreciation of human experience.

PART 03

CULTURAL CONTINUITY

From the cautionary rhymes of Ông Ba Bị to riverbank warnings like Ma Da, Vietnamese legends reflect centuries of agrarian life along rivers, bamboo groves, and rice paddies.

We hope VN FOLKS serves as a useful educational resource for anyone interested in exploring Vietnamese culture, mythology, and science.
