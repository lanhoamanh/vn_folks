ABOUT VNFOLKS

“Exploring the intersection between cultural folklore and scientific understanding.”

“VN FOLKS began with a straightforward goal: to document Vietnamese supernatural legends and traditional lore while exploring the physical and psychological science behind them.

Across Vietnam, generations have shared stories about nighttime spirits, animal omens, celestial events, and protective household rituals. Some originated to explain natural phenomena before modern science, others served as community safety guidelines, and many evolved into cherished seasonal traditions.

This archive provides accessible, nuanced context for these tales without dismissing traditional beliefs or oversimplifying historical culture.”
