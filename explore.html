Folklore & Science
Hiện Tượng Bóng Đè

Nighttime Chest Pressure (Bóng Đè)

In Vietnamese folk culture, waking up unable to move while sensing a heavy chest pressure was known as Bóng Đè. While traditional tales attributed this to wandering night spirits, modern sleep science explains it as a temporary disconnect during REM sleep.

Astronomy & Sky Lore
Nhật Thực & Nguyệt Thực

Solar & Lunar Eclipses

Early Vietnamese villages viewed eclipses as terrifying cosmic events where giant sky beasts attempted to devour the sun or moon—prompting hamlets to bang brass pestles and drums to rescue the sky.

Wildlife & Human Perception
Điềm Báo Từ Động Vật

Animal Instincts & Night Omens

Traditional Vietnamese lore linked animal behavior—such as the call of the barn owl or dogs barking into night shadows—to upcoming events. Modern biology reveals how sensory perception drives these animal habits.

Tradition & Empathy
Cô Hồn & Xá Tội Vong Nhân

Hungry Ghosts & The Seventh Lunar Month

During the Seventh Lunar Month, ceremonies are dedicated to Cô Hồn—wandering souls without surviving kin. This tradition blends ancestral respect, Buddhist compassion, and community solidarity.

History & Constellations
Thiên Văn Trong Thần Thoại Việt

Sky Lore & Ancient Astronomy

Long before modern telescopes, Vietnamese communities wove astronomical observations into rich folklore—from Chú Cuội sitting beneath the lunar banyan tree to monsoon star markers on ancient bronze drums.

Seasons & Heritage
Phong Tục & Tín Ngưỡng Ngày Tết

Tết Customs & Seasonal Renewal

Tết Nguyên Đán represents a major cultural reset rooted in agrarian survival, home renewal, and community harmony. Customs like erecting the Cây Nêu bamboo pole combine spiritual tradition with practical spring preparation.
